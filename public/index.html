How it works

ProcureIQ works in three simple steps

No setup, no integration, no IT department required.

  1. 1

    Upload your supplier quotes

    Any format, any structure. PDFs, spreadsheets, email attachments — drop them in as they arrived.

  2. 2

    ProcureIQ extracts, normalizes and compares

    It checks pricing, specifications, freight, MOQ, payment terms, lead times, compliance information and other commercial risks.

  3. 3

    Download your savings report

    A clear recommendation you can take straight to your CFO, with the questions to send back to each supplier.

Why I built this

Before founding Adora Innovations, I was an executive assistant. I sat next to executives and watched how companies actually operate on the inside.

I saw procurement managers drowning in manual, repetitive work that AI should have automated long ago. The pain is real, and validated by the procurement professionals we have spoken to so far.

They compare quotes in Excel, and it takes hours. They have to dig out hidden costs, check specifications, compare delivery and payment terms, reconcile prices quoted in different formats, and weigh up supplier risk. They build those comparison tables from scratch every single week, with zero institutional memory of what happened last time.

I understand that world intimately. Not because I studied procurement — because I lived inside the operations for years, and I know what broken workflows look like from the inside.

That is my angle, and I am here to serve procurement teams.
